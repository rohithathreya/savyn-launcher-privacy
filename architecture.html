The picture

Savyn is not a chatbot with a memory dump. It is a domain of life — Fitness, Chess, Food — holding threads of continuity. A thread is a lens: Nutrition under Food, Training under Fitness. You dump what's on your mind; the threads that actually moved write a beat that is the episode.

Four jobs are kept separate on purpose. When they collapse into one voice, the product becomes a clerk: "paste the game," "keep going," "you skipped vision." The whole system is built to refuse that.

Layers of memory

Domain

A life area

Fitness, Chess, Food. Each has a North Star — how you want to live here (want, avoid, when). At domain height lives a domain-next roster: one line per work thread, each with an expected timestamp when that thread has an honest clock. The door prioritizes the closest timestamp. It is not a chore list.

Thread

A continuity lens

Nutrition under Food; Training under Fitness. Not a topic tag, not today's anecdote. Each thread carries a beat-structure blurb (what to infer from a dump), a thin so-far spine folded forward on each beat, filed episodes, and its one next line on the domain roster.

Episode

The dump

One typed moment a thread could file. You spoke; classify named this lens; the lens wrote the beat. That beat is the episode — stored and embedded immediately. Memory must not wait for Sync.

Do taps are not episodes. They open a URL or app. Never a turn, never a beat, never a thread line.

How a sitting runs

1 · The starter door

You open an app in a domain (Swiggy at 6pm) or the domain tile itself. Before you've spoken, Savyn owes a callout: one short line keyed by (domain, app). It sees the app name, the time, and the soonest domain-next lines — not today's recaps, not the whole North Star. Chips only if a real Do exists. Otherwise silence — you type.

2 · You sit

You type. That text is a user turn. Classify names every thread this move actually moved. Up to a handful write in parallel — each following its own blurb, inferring without clarifying questions. On that same call: episode stored, so-far folded, domain-next + clock rewritten.

3 · The continuation door

Once there's a typed dump and the sitting isn't synced yet, the starter is used up. A continuation callout follows — a notice, not a hello, not a recap of what you just said. Do taps do not trigger this.

4 · Sync — leaving the room

You merge, or quiet closes the sitting after ~2 hours. Sync stamps the sitting closed, wipes the chorus transcript, and drops the per-app callout cache so the next open sees fresh nexts. It does not re-read beats, rewrite so-far, or mint new threads. Hide is not Sync.

Browsing lunch options? Before you pick, quietly wait and watch cravings arise and pass, just like the late night urges.

A door line — idea or question, never homework

The door

The starter is an app-hour orientation, not a sitting recap. The packet is deliberately small: current time, domain title, app display name if known, a clipped North Star (usually ignored), and a handful of soonest domain-next lines.

  • Keyed by app — Swiggy ≠ Hevy ≠ chess.com. One cache per domain + app.
  • A day is 6am → 6am, local. Rewritten lazily on open when stale (~1 hour) or day-part flips.
  • North Star may contribute one clause — only when this app + this clock make that clause live (Swiggy at 1am × avoid late-night snacking).
  • Never invented lunch, fiber, or a 7:00 for a thread with no clock. Never skip-guilt. Never a sitting essay.

The two likely typed moves ("Suggest a meal," "Log my order") cycle as composer placeholders — not chips. Sit as a tap on the door is retired.

Threads & beats

One dump is not a broadcast. Classify decides which threads speak. If you replied to one member of the sitting, only that thread answers.

Each thread writer may retrieve:

  • The dump, verbatim, and any prior dump still on this sitting
  • This thread's so-far, next, clock, live ideas, and blurb
  • What this thread already filed earlier in this sitting
  • Recent group turns (faces, not a second copy of the dump)

Each thread writer must not:

  • See other threads' private so-far
  • Invent facts or clocks
  • Echo the dump as the face, paste notation, or use clerk voice
  • Ask them to fill the structure as a form

If the model fails or the face is just a synonym of the dump, Savyn salvages from this thread's next or so-far, or stays silent. Empty is better than a polite recap.

Chips

The door is a line. A chip is only allowed if it is a Do: a real object in the room they can open right now. At most two. Live card first, then pantry. If neither serves a current next, show nothing.

Do

Opens a URL or app. Chosen from things the domain already has — a live search result parked after merge, or links they saved to this domain's pantry. The language model is forbidden from inventing URLs. When the line names a specific object, that noun matches against live card and pantry. If nothing in the room is that object, silence.

What chips are not

Three synonyms of the liner. Recovery homework. A second composer. "Open Hevy" when they already opened Hevy from the widget. If a chip cannot open a real object, it should not exist.

Every Do chip makes two things obvious: the move ("Caro lesson," "Play 10+0") and where it opens (YouTube, Chess.com, Hevy). Tapping Do launches that destination — not a turn, not a beat.

Objects in the room

Search exists for one reason: next named a thing in the world, and a practitioner would Google that thing. After a dump rewrites domain-nexts:

  • If next is vague ("keep training," "check in") — stay silent. No search.
  • If next is a named object — at most one query, resolve it, park a live card for chips only.
  • The Open Graph title is not the chip. The verb comes from what the page is.

The pantry is their shelf for this domain — links they saved on purpose. The system never writes the pantry for them. Associated apps are mapped to the domain; opening one from the widget is the sitting. That launch is not a chip.

When Savyn shuts up

Intelligence here is mostly knowing when to shut up.

Situation What Savyn does
Callout has no lived noun Reject the line; fall back to a next, not a welcome
No Do that serves a next Silence — do not invent a link or fill with Sit taps
Classify finds no thread Say nothing rather than recap the roster
A thread would only echo the dump Silent, or salvage from that thread's next / so-far
Search has nothing named No live card
Overlay hidden Sitting remains. Memory already landed on the dump

The product prefers a thin true memory to a thick invented one.

Each writer has one job, one packet, one shape — and a silence that is cheaper than a lie.

One sitting, end to end

They open Swiggy at 6pm from Food.

  • The starter door is the Swiggy line: time, app name, soonest Food domain-nexts. No Sit chips. Says cycle in the composer. A Do only if a real object is in the room.
  • They type a dump. Classify names Nutrition. Nutrition writes the episode beat, folds so-far, rewrites its domain-next + lunch clock. Game analysis stays quiet. Then a continuation on the sitting — not a rewrite of the Swiggy cache.
  • A Do tap only opens the URL. No chorus.
  • They leave. Overlay hides. The sitting is still open. Memory already landed.
  • When they Sync, chat clears. The Food callout cache is dropped. Next Swiggy open writes a fresh line from the new domain-next roster.

That is the intelligence: the door names the move on this app this hour from the closest next, the threads read and remember on the dump, Sync only leaves the room, and chips are only real objects this life can open.
