Who we are

Savyn is developed by Savyn Labs. It is an Android OS layer: a home widget and sitting overlay organized as domains of life (Fitness, Food, Chess, and so on), each holding continuity threads. It is not a cloud notebook. It is not a social network.

How it works

Personal state lives in local storage on your device: domain North Stars, thread blurbs, so-far spines, filed episodes, domain-next clocks, pantry links, associated apps, sitting transcripts, and on-device embeddings (BGE) used to retrieve your own episodes.

When a writer must run — classify, beat, door callout, pantry pick, live-object query — Savyn sends an anonymized packet through our proxy to an AI provider. The packet is the job described in Architecture: one writer, one view, nothing extra. Conversation history and lifetime episodes do not leave the device as a dump.

Your phone→ Savyn proxy→ AI provider→ Your phone

We do not keep a database of your sittings. The proxy exists to keep API keys off the device — it forwards the request and returns the result. Nothing is logged or persisted as user content.

What lives on your device

  • Domains and North Stars — how you want to live in each life area
  • Threads — blurbs, so-far, next + clock, ideas, episode history
  • Sittings — typed dumps and chorus faces until you Sync or quiet-close
  • Callout cache — per (domain, app) door lines, local
  • Pantry — links you saved to a domain, never auto-written by search
  • Associated apps — the apps you mapped to a domain from the widget
  • Embeddings — on-device vectors of your episodes for retrieval
  • Portraits and generated art — stored locally

We cannot access, view, or retrieve this. We have no user-content database.

AI services

Cloud models are used only for the write-path jobs (classify, beats, doors, link-about, pantry pick, quiet-sync gate, live-object pick). Requests go through our proxy to OpenAI and, for some writers, Groq. Before anything leaves the device, names, exact addresses, and contact details are stripped or generalized.

Voice

If you use voice input, audio is streamed to a transcription service for speech-to-text in that moment. Audio is not retained. The resulting text stays on your device as a dump like any other typed turn.

Live objects

If a thread’s next names a real object in the world, Savyn may run one search query and park a single live card for Do chips. That card is not written into your pantry. Open Graph metadata may be fetched for a URL you already have. Queries are practitioner search strings — not your sitting transcript.

Share links

If you choose to share a card, Savyn can publish an unlisted snapshot to our share service. The snapshot expires. Links are noindex. We do not attach your identity. You control whether a card is shared at all.

No training on your data

We use API-tier access. Per provider terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train their models.

What we don’t do

  • No account required — full product without signing up
  • No ads, pixels, or ad SDKs
  • No analytics telemetry — no Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or equivalent
  • No selling data — we have nothing to sell
  • No silent pantry writes — search never saves links for you
  • No cloud backup we control — uninstall and local memory is gone

Permissions

Usage access

Lets Savyn see which apps you open and when, so a sitting can key the door by the app you actually launched (Swiggy vs Hevy vs chess.com). You grant this explicitly in Android Settings. Raw usage timelines stay on the device. Only anonymized, job-sized context (app display name, local time) is sent with a door packet.

Microphone

Voice dumps only while you are capturing. Never in the background.

Notifications

Optional check-ins and reminders you control. No marketing notifications.

Alarms

On-device Android alarms for times you request.

Internet

Required for AI writers, live-object search, optional share links, and Google Play billing. Personal state does not ride along beyond the anonymized packet for that job.

Default / widget / overlay

Savyn can be your home widget and sitting overlay. That is a launcher capability you grant. It does not send your home layout to us.

Subscriptions & billing

Optional paid plans are managed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not process payments and do not receive card numbers or billing addresses — only entitlement status (active / expired).

Your control

  • Uninstall — all local sittings, threads, embeddings, pantry, gone
  • Sync / hide — hide leaves the sitting open; Sync clears the chorus. Memory already filed on the dump either way
  • Thread book — you edit blurbs, next lines, and North Star without a model
  • Revoke permissions — usage access, microphone, notifications in Android Settings
  • Share expiry — unlisted share snapshots expire; you need not share at all

Children’s privacy

Savyn is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly process data from children.

Policy updates

If we update this policy, we’ll note the changes here and update the date above. Significant changes will be communicated in the app.
