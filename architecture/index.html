Sitting
intelligence

How Savyn thinks in a life. What a thread remembers. How a dump becomes an episode. Why the door speaks from the soonest next — and why a chip is only allowed if it opens something real.

The picture

Savyn is not a chatbot with a memory dump. It is a domain of life — Fitness, Chess, Food — holding threads of continuity. A thread is a lens. You dump. The lenses that actually moved write a beat. That beat is the episode.

Four jobs stay separate on purpose. Collapse them into one voice and the product becomes a clerk: paste the game, keep going, you skipped vision. The system is built to refuse that.

Callout
The door

What you’d be doing right now on this app — short, lived, not a dump. Speaks from the soonest domain-next, not the whole board.

Threads
The reading

Notice what this dump meant for this path, following that thread’s beat-structure blurb — not a generic recap of the sitting.

The beat
Durable memory

On the dump: write the episode, fold so-far, rewrite domain-next and clock. Sync only leaves the room. It does not write memory.

Links & apps
Objects in the room

A real next they can tap — a video, a board, a lesson. Attached after the line. Never invented from thin air.

Layers of memory

Domain

A life area

Fitness, Chess, Food. Each holds a North Star — how you want to live here: want, avoid, when. At domain height lives a roster: one line per work thread, with an honest clock when the dump implies one. The door prioritizes the closest timestamp. It is not a chore list.

Thread

A continuity lens

Nutrition under Food. Training under Fitness. Not a topic tag. Not today’s anecdote. Each carries a beat-structure blurb, a thin so-far spine, filed episodes, and its one next on the domain roster.

Episode

The dump

One typed moment a thread could file. You spoke. Classify named this lens. The lens wrote the beat. Stored and embedded immediately. Memory must not wait for Sync.

Do taps are not episodes. They open a URL or an app. Never a turn. Never a beat. Never a thread line.

How a sitting runs

01 — Starter

The door before you’ve spoken

You open an app in a domain, or the domain itself. Savyn owes one line, keyed by domain and app. It sees the time, the app name, and the soonest nexts — not today’s recaps, not the whole North Star. Chips only if a real Do exists. Otherwise silence. You type.

02 — Sit

Classify, then write

You type. Classify names every thread this move actually moved. A handful write in parallel, each following its own blurb, inferring without a clarifying question. On that same call: episode stored, so-far folded, next and clock rewritten.

03 — Continue

A notice, not a hello

Once there’s a typed dump and the sitting isn’t synced, the starter is used up. A continuation follows — the next beat in their nouns, not a recap of the paragraph they just handed. Do taps do not trigger this.

04 — Sync

Leaving the room

You merge, or quiet closes after about two hours. Chat clears. The per-app door cache drops so the next open sees the nexts that just changed. Sync does not re-read beats, rewrite so-far, or mint threads. Hide is not Sync.

Browsing lunch options? Before you pick, quietly wait and watch cravings arise and pass, just like the late night urges.

A door line — idea or question. Never homework.

The door

The starter is an app-hour orientation, not a sitting recap. The packet is deliberately small: current time, domain title, app display name if known, a clipped North Star — usually ignored — and a handful of soonest domain-next lines.

  • Keyed by app. Swiggy is not Hevy is not chess.com. One cache per domain + app.
  • A day is 6am to 6am, local. Rewritten lazily on open when stale, or when the day-part flips.
  • North Star may contribute one clause — only when this app and this clock make that clause live.
  • Never an invented lunch, fiber, or 7:00 for a thread with no clock. Never skip-guilt. Never a sitting essay.

The two likely typed moves cycle as composer placeholders. Sit as a tap on the door is retired. If they want to answer the line, they type.

Threads & beats

One dump is not a broadcast. Classify decides which threads speak. If you replied to one member of the sitting, only that thread answers.

A thread may retrieve

  • The dump, verbatim, and any prior dump still on this sitting
  • This thread’s so-far, next, clock, live ideas, and blurb
  • What this thread already filed earlier in this sitting
  • Recent group turns — faces, not a second copy of the dump

A thread must not

  • See other threads’ private so-far
  • Invent facts or clocks
  • Echo the dump as the face, paste notation, or use clerk voice
  • Ask them to fill the structure as a form

If the model fails, or the face is a synonym of the dump, Savyn salvages from that thread’s next or so-far — or stays silent. Empty is better than a polite recap.

Chips

The door is a line. A chip is only allowed if it is a Do: a real object in the room they can open right now. At most two. Live card first, then pantry. If neither serves a current next, show nothing.

Do

Opens a URL or an app. Chosen from things the domain already has — a live result parked after merge, or links they saved to this domain’s pantry. The model is forbidden from inventing URLs. When the line names a specific object, that noun matches against live card and pantry. If nothing in the room is that object: silence.

What chips are not

Three synonyms of the liner. Recovery homework. A second composer. “Open Hevy” when they already opened Hevy from the widget. If a chip cannot open a real object, it should not exist.

Every Do makes two things obvious: the move — Caro lesson, Play 10+0 — and where it opens. Tapping launches that destination. Not a turn. Not a beat.

Objects in the room

Search exists for one reason: next named a thing in the world, and a practitioner would Google that thing.

  • If next is vague — keep training, check in — stay silent. No search.
  • If next is a named object: at most one query, resolve it, park a live card for chips only.
  • The Open Graph title is not the chip. The verb comes from what the page is.

The pantry is their shelf for this domain — links they saved on purpose. The system never writes the pantry for them. Opening an associated app from the widget is the sitting. That launch is not a chip.

When Savyn shuts up

Intelligence here is mostly knowing when to shut up.

Callout has no lived noun
Reject the line. Fall back to a next, not a welcome.
No Do that serves a next
Silence. Do not invent a link or fill with Sit taps.
Classify finds no thread
Say nothing rather than recap the roster.
A thread would only echo the dump
Silent — or salvage from that thread’s next / so-far.
Search has nothing named
No live card.
Overlay hidden
Sitting remains. Memory already landed on the dump.

A thin true memory over a thick invented one.

Each writer has one job, one packet, one shape — and a silence that is cheaper than a lie.

One sitting, end to end

They open Swiggy at 6pm from Food.

  • The starter door is the Swiggy line: time, app name, soonest Food nexts. No Sit chips. Says cycle in the composer. A Do only if a real object is in the room.
  • They type a dump. Classify names Nutrition. Nutrition writes the episode, folds so-far, rewrites its next and lunch clock. Game analysis stays quiet. Then a continuation on the sitting — not a rewrite of the Swiggy cache.
  • A Do tap only opens the URL. No chorus.
  • They leave. Overlay hides. The sitting is still open. Memory already landed.
  • When they Sync, chat clears. The Food callout cache is dropped. Next Swiggy open writes a fresh line from the new roster.

That is the intelligence: the door names the move on this app this hour from the closest next. The threads read and remember on the dump. Sync only leaves the room. Chips are only real objects this life can open.
