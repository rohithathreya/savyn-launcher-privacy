Apps have their own agenda. Your phone is yours.

You are trying to close the gap between who you are and who you want to be. But right now, the threads of your life are scattered across a passive grid, pushed and pulled by algorithms while your device just watches.

Savyn takes your side. It is an OS fiercely biased toward your life—connecting your context and aligning the glass to exactly where you are going.

01 // Speak once

One sentence. The phone reshapes.

Hold the mic. Say "working on my Caro-Kann". Chess opens with the right lines, the right videos, the right next move — already loaded. You didn't open an app. You didn't write a prompt. You spoke once.

02 // On device

One responsibility, live on the glass — specific lines, the exact references, the apps you'd reach for. Not a notification. A next move.

03 // Agents

Your phone gets a bench of operators.

Music. Chess. Mornings. Eating. The way you think. Each agent owns a domain of your life — and runs many responsibilities under it, so you never start from zero and no idea dies alone in a notes app.

04 // Responsibilities

Each agent holds many responsibilities — not one.

Under Chess: Caro-Kann, blitz warmup, game review. Under mornings: coffee, meditation, the gym. Each responsibility is an arc you're actually on. Savyn watches them with you, stacks every moment you forge, and surfaces the right one when you're in it.

05 // On device

Responsibilities (5) under one agent — you're in Caro-Kann now; the rest wait until you switch arcs. Tap Complete and the moment stacks into that responsibility's spine.

06 // Continuations

Two days in, a real continuation lands.

"Try the …Bf5 setup against the Advance — here's a 4-minute breakdown." Specific. Linked. Forward-moving. Drawn from every moment you've put into that responsibility — never a generic nudge from a streak engine.

07 // Progress

No streaks. No scores. Just the mass of moments you forged.

Every responsibility keeps a chronological spine — stacked, dated, undeniable. The shape of who you're becoming, kept faithfully and never gamified.

08 // How it works

Momentum.
Not management.

01

Zero activation energy.

One tap, one sentence. Savyn parses raw intent in milliseconds — no menus, no app drawer, no prompt-engineering yourself.

02

03
