Apps have their own agenda. Your phone is yours.

You're closing the gap — between who you are and who you want to be.
But the threads of your life are scattered. Pushed by algorithms. Pulled by feeds. Your phone just watches.
Savyn takes your side. An OS biased toward your life — aligning the glass to where you're going.

01 A simple cue — "lunch at 2; plug my gaps" — my phone will get back to me at the right time with exactly what I need. Highly contextual and personalized.

02 Builds deep knowledge of my music interests and what I like when — down to the mood, artist, genre — every last detail.

03 Everything I say is understood not as standalone statements but as part of arcs. One example is my running arc. Nothing I say is ever lost or misinterpreted.

01 // Speak once

One sentence. The phone reshapes.

Hold the mic. Say "something different for dinner tonight". The home screen aligns to your diet, your constraints, the cravings you've been hinting at all week — already loaded. You didn't open an app. You didn't write a prompt. You spoke once.

02 // Agents

Your phone gets a bench of operators.

Music. Fitness. Mornings. Eating. The way you think. Each agent owns a domain of your life — and runs many responsibilities under it, so you never start from zero and no idea dies alone in a notes app.

03 // Responsibilities

Each agent holds many responsibilities — not one.

Under Fitness: running, recovery, strength. Under mornings: coffee, meditation, the first hour. Each responsibility is an arc you're actually on. Savyn watches them with you, stacks every moment you forge, and surfaces the right one when you're in it.

04 // Continuations

Two days in, a real continuation lands.

"Easy 4k tomorrow — your knee was off after Tuesday's run. Flat route, recovery zone only. Five-minute mobility flow before you head out." Specific. Linked. Forward-moving. Drawn from every moment you've put into that responsibility — never a generic nudge from a streak engine.

05 // Progress

No streaks. No scores. Just the mass of moments you forged.

Every responsibility keeps a chronological spine — stacked, dated, undeniable. The shape of who you're becoming, kept faithfully and never gamified.

06 // How it works

Momentum.
Not management.

01

Zero activation energy.

One tap, one sentence. Savyn parses raw intent in milliseconds — no menus, no app drawer, no prompt-engineering yourself.

02

The OS bends to the moment.

Switch responsibility and the phone changes shape — apps, widgets, links, all materialized for the arc you're actually in.

03

Your ideas compound.

Every moment slots into the right responsibility under the right agent. Savyn hands your best concepts back when that context returns.

04

Private by build.

All storage on device. AI calls are anonymous, routed through secure proxies. Read the privacy policy.

07 // Join the alpha

Speak once. It begins.

Android closed alpha. Limited seats. No marketing — only sequence updates.
